# Pain Points - Skilled Nursing, Long-Term Care & Assisted Living

These are the day-to-day pressures Spectrum dx PCR testing speaks to. Use them to make
emails specific and relevant - one pain point per follow-up.

## Antibiotic overuse and stewardship pressure
UTIs are one of the most common infections in long-term care, and empiric antibiotic
treatment based on non-specific symptoms drives unnecessary antibiotic use and
resistance. CMS and CDC push antibiotic stewardship programs. Same-day, pathogen-specific
UTI PCR supports more targeted therapy and helps meet stewardship goals.

## Infection control and surveillance burden
Facilities carry heavy infection-prevention and surveillance requirements, with survey
exposure when programs fall short. Rapid colonization screening (C. auris, CRE) supports
surveillance, cohorting, and prevention before an outbreak spreads.

## Emerging drug-resistant threats
Candida auris and carbapenem-resistant Enterobacterales (CRE) are rising threats in
long-term care and are difficult to detect and contain. Public health departments track
lab testing capacity; fast molecular colonization screening helps facilities act quickly.

## Slow reference-lab turnaround
Sending specimens to a reference lab and waiting days delays treatment and infection-
control decisions. The value of same-day PCR is turning results around fast enough to
actually change a clinical or infection-control decision.

## Respiratory season strain
Flu, COVID-19, and other respiratory pathogens move fast through congregate settings.
Fast identification guides treatment and infection-control response during outbreaks.

## Resident safety and survey readiness
Administrators and directors of nursing balance resident safety, staffing, and regulatory
survey readiness. Diagnostic speed and clarity reduce guesswork and support defensible
clinical and infection-control decisions.

## Facility types and nuances
- Skilled Nursing Facilities (SNF): high UTI volume, strong stewardship/survey pressure.
- Assisted Living / Memory Care: outbreak vigilance among vulnerable residents.
- Adult Day Health Centers: quick screening to prevent spread among at-risk older adults.
- Home Health / Hospice: care coordinated across many residences; fast answers matter.
