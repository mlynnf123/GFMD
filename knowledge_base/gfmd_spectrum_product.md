# Spectrum dx Molecular Diagnostics - PCR Testing for SNF, LTC & Assisted Living

Spectrum dx (Spectrum Molecular Diagnostics Laboratory) is an Orange County, California
laboratory offering rapid, pathogen-specific PCR testing built specifically for skilled
nursing, long-term care, and assisted living settings. The core promise is same-day
results instead of empiric guesswork - turning results around fast enough to change a
clinical or infection-control decision.

## Who it is for
Administrators, directors of nursing, infection preventionists, medical directors, and
clinical staff at skilled nursing facilities (SNF), long-term care (LTC), assisted living
communities, memory care, adult day health centers, home health, and hospice agencies.

## Test Menu

### UTI PCR (Urinary Tract Infection Testing)
Same-day, pathogen-specific results to support more targeted treatment decisions and
reduce reliance on empiric antibiotic therapy. Directly supports antibiotic stewardship
programs, which are a regulatory and clinical priority in long-term care.

### C. auris PCR (Drug-Resistant Yeast Screening)
Rapid colonization screening for infection-control and surveillance programs. Spectrum
Molecular Diagnostics Laboratory is listed among laboratories with C. auris testing
capacity on the April 2025 laboratory list published by the LA County Department of
Public Health - a third-party credibility signal.

### CRE Screening (Drug-Resistant "Superbug" Screening)
Colonization screening for carbapenem-resistant Enterobacterales (CRE) to support
infection prevention, surveillance, and resident management. CRE is an escalating threat
in long-term care.

### Mpox PCR (Monkeypox Testing)
Molecular testing to detect mpox virus (MPXV) and help facilities implement appropriate
clinical and infection-control measures quickly.

### Onychomycosis PCR (Toenail Fungus Testing)
Rapid identification of fungal pathogens associated with nail infections to support
podiatry and treatment decisions.

### Respiratory PCR (Flu, COVID-19 & Other Respiratory Infections)
Fast identification of common respiratory pathogens to guide treatment and
infection-control decisions, especially during respiratory season.
